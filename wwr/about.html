Rapids Trees

At Rapids Trees, our mission is to guide adventurers through unforgettable white water rapids surrounded by pristine forest scenery, combining safety, teamwork, and respect for nature on every trip.

History

Founded in 2012 by a small team of experienced river guides and forestry enthusiasts, Rapids Trees began with just two rafts, a hand-drawn map of the canyon, and a deep respect for wild mountain rivers. What started as weekend expeditions for local hikers quickly grew as travelers from all over came to experience our signature routes surrounded by towering pine and cedar forests. Over the past decade, we have expanded our fleet and trained a dedicated crew of certified guides while staying true to our roots—prioritizing river safety, small-group hospitality, and active conservation of the watersheds we call home.
